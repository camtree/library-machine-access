## Search guidance

A publication may appear relevant to more than one topic.

Collections reflect programmes, institutions, projects, journals, geographical contexts, and curated themes. Collection membership should therefore be used as contextual information rather than as a complete subject classification. For broad enquiries, search across the whole repository as well as within relevant collections.

Agents should take account of the date of publication as policy context may have changed since research has taken place.

Agents should take account of the geographical location as practice may be influenced by local policy and culture.

Items described in the dc:type of item metadata as 'Research Reports' are more comprehensive than those described as 'Research Summaries'.

For broader questions:
1. search the full repository first
2. use collection-scoped searches to refine the evidence
3. preserve collection context in the final answer
